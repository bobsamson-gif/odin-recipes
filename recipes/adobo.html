Filipino Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken Adobo is a classic Filipino dish braised in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and bay leaves. It's savory, tangy, and tastes even better the next day!

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken thighs
  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • ⅓ cup white vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil

Steps

  1. In a bowl, combine soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, onion, bay leaves, and peppercorns.
  2. Add chicken and marinate for at least 30 minutes (or overnight).
  3. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Brown chicken on both sides.
  4. Pour in the marinade and bring to a boil. Lower heat, cover, and simmer 25–30 minutes until chicken is tender.
  5. Remove lid and simmer another 5–10 minutes to thicken the sauce.
  6. Serve hot with steamed rice. Enjoy!
