Chocolate Cake

Description

A classic moist chocolate cake topped with smooth chocolate buttercream frosting. Simple to make and absolutely delicious!

Ingredients

Cake:

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ⅓ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 5 tbsp vegetable oil

Frosting:

  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • ⅔ cup cocoa powder
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour an 8-inch round pan.
  2. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Add water, vanilla, vinegar, and oil. Whisk until smooth.
  4. Pour into pan and bake 30–35 minutes. Let cool completely.
  5. For frosting: beat butter until smooth. Stir in cocoa and powdered sugar. Add milk and vanilla; beat until creamy.
  6. Spread frosting over cooled cake. Slice and enjoy!
